Grow Your Stake
===============

Cardano Stakepool Operator

![](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4046923609-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPQmCVki2WHg9QcW9pdrX%252Fuploads%252F7c1d9GZG5dTf42qX2pB9%252FPartners_GrowYourStake.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D9be10e8a-3c54-436b-9610-1135137050a8&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=7924c1aa&sv=2)

Grow Your Stake is a Cardano mission-driven stake pool. They have partnered with Drop4drop and use 100% profits from their pool operator rewards to fund water wells in places around the world that need it most. Their first well was completed recently in Uganda, which brings clean water to a village of about 400 people.

Cornucopias plans to also delegate to GYS, as the more delegation, the more pool rewards, which leads to more wells.
