Consensus 2022
==============

In June 2022 we took 7 members of Cornucopias to the annual Consenus event in Austin Texas.

Some of the highlights are as follows:

* Charles Hoskinson CEO of IOHK mentioned Cornucopias by name in his [Cardano keynote](https://mobile.twitter.com/CornucopiasGame/status/1535002179957841920) on the pre-Consensus Cardano special evening (the night before Consenus)
* Charles Hoskinson mentioned Cornucopias by name in a [Yahoo Finance](https://mobile.twitter.com/YahooFinance/status/1534909339613413378) interview
* Josh and Rob did a 30 minute Cornucopias presentation where they:

  + Talked through a video showing off the UE5 graphics for the upcoming game
  + Presented the Desktop Launcher and showed/hinted at future functionality
  + [Rob Greig](mailto:undefined) played through a demo of the Home Bubble Building System
  + [Josh Jones](mailto:undefined) and [Josh Miller](https://twitter.com/RobGreig3/status/1536004352380387330) of IOHK, played an early eSports demo of a GTi Javelin racing game
  + Spoke about [planting real trees](https://mobile.twitter.com/krissbaird/status/1535708542765760513) with [Veritree](/the-company/partners/partners-metaverse/veritree)
* Met a lot of Cardano Influencers including BigPey, Kaizen Crypto
* Spoke to a lot of Cornucopias Supporters and NFT holders
* Held a mini Javelin racing competition which was won by Damon of Charli3 with a time of 1:46s and was also entered by the President of IOHK - [Tamara Haasen](https://iohk.io/en/team/tamara-haasen)
* Handed out exclusive Cornucopias T-Shirts
* Attended a number of Cardano and Community evening events
* Hosted a number of private business networking lunches
* Spoke to a number of Cardano Projects with a view to working on future collaborations/partnerships with a number of them
* Spoke to the Cardano Foundation with a view to future collaborations/initiatives
* Spoke to the Project Catalyst team with a view to working on Cornucopias community projects in the future (not about raising funds via Catalyst)
