Web3Expo 2022
=============

In October 2022 the 4x Founders of Cornucopias attended the [Web3Expo](https://web3expo.live/) (W3BX) hosted in the Wynn Hotel in Las Vegas, Nevada.

![](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4046923609-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPQmCVki2WHg9QcW9pdrX%252Fuploads%252F8R3YHK7YGOhTCqxf1ylk%252Fimage.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D4b577c39-6b03-4def-966d-4cdabeb321be&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=5d761803&sv=2)

The Cardano Foundation had its own conference room where various project leads from the Cardano ecosystem that had attended [cNFTcon](/gameplay/community/status-updates-archive/status-updates-2022/october-2022/cnftcon-2022) were invited as VIPs to speak at the event.

The Co-CEO of Cornucopias [Rob Greig](mailto:undefined) sat on a 5x man panel and discussed the topic of Web3 sustainability.

The event which was mainly for the Bitcoin and Ethereum community was top-billed by Kevin O'Leary "Mr. Wonderful" from the USA show Shark Tank. The Cardano Community was there though and we showed up in numbers. Maybe next year we will have a larger presence?
