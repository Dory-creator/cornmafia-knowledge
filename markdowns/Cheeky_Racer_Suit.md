Cheeky Racer Suit
=================

Race in Style with your very own racing suit

Our first ever Asset Partner collaboration was with the YouTube channel [Cheeky Crypto](https://www.youtube.com/@CheekyCrypto).

A snapshot of ADA (₳) = $0.263 and Cornucopias $COPI = $0.014 @ 1500UTC on 19th December 2022​ was noted - the prices were used in calculating the price of the Assets in ADA (₳) and $COPI.

**Maximum Supply:** 5,560

**Minted:** Tuesday 20th December 2022

**Mint Price 1 :** 7 ADA (₳) + 1,285 $COPI ($20)

**or Mint Price 2:** 91 ADA ($24)

**Delivery Method:** via Cornucopias internally built marketplace​​

**Secondary Market:** [**https://www.jpg.store/collection/cornucopias-cheeky-racer**](https://www.jpg.store/collection/cornucopias-cheeky-racer)

#### Colors and Rarities (the base of the Asset represents the rarity color)

* White Base - Common (50% rarity)
* Green Base - Uncommon (25% rarity)
* Blue Base - Rare (15% rarity)
* Purple Base - Legendary (7.5% rarity)
* Gold Base - Mythic (2.5% rarity)

### Utility

The Racing Suit will have the following utility within Cornucopias

* It is an **item of clothing** that can be **worn** by your character in-game whilst racing.
* Access to Cheeky Crypto discord and partner is given along with discount off Cheeky Crypto merchandise for the Asset holders.
* There will be exclusive races for Cheeky Racing Suit Asset holders.
* May offer better racing performance due to streamlined racing suit (yet to be decided).

  + As an [Asset](/blockchain/player-owned-assets), it will form part of your Cornucopias Asset **collection.**
* **You own the** Asset **asset and will be able to sell or trade it.**
