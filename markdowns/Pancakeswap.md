PancakeSwap
===========

You will need a MetaMask wallet to use PancakeSwap

On Friday 10th December 2021 @ 9am EST the Cornucopias [$COPI](/blockchain/usdcopi-token) token launched on PancakeSwap.

![](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4046923609-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPQmCVki2WHg9QcW9pdrX%252Fuploads%252FvVKVcqZPMMHjzr5V5SfN%252Fimage.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D026c1a47-bd3a-40c3-a107-46498e96da16&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=6b00cca4&sv=2)

PancakeSwap is a decentralized finance (DeFi) blockchain app that enables its users to swap crypto tokens. The PancakeSwap platform allows users to trade without requiring them to go through a centralized exchange. The PancakeSwap platform aims to provide an opportunity for users to trade and earn crypto on the **BNB chain (formally known as the Binance Smart Chain - BSC)** within seconds.

Since PancakeSwap functions on ‌the BNB blockchain, it charges low transaction fees.

**Download MetaMask:** <https://metamask.io/>

**Add BSC to MetaMask:** <https://academy.binance.com/en/articles/connecting-metamask-to-binance-smart-chain.amp>

**Exchange:** <https://pancakeswap.finance/swap>

**Coin Market Cap Profile:** <https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/cornucopias/>

**Cornucopias BSC Contract:** 0xfea292e5ea4510881bdb840e3cec63abd43f936f
