Core Dynamics Astro IV
======================

The Core Dynamics Astro IV is a flying vehicle that is used to travel from your Sector Hub to the [Themed Zones](/gameplay/themed-zones) or the [City](/gameplay/city-life). It can also be used for racing and is a Racing Class C vehicle.
