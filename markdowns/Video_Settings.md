Video Settings
==============

You might find this useful as a reference when navigating the in-game video settings. The list of options can be rather intimidating if you are not familiar with them. Hopefully this helps.

Scruffy walks you through the video settings in the game.

### Definitions

**FPS - Frames per second**
This determines how smooth your gameplay looks and feels. Higher FPS equals smoother play.
Lower settings often boost FPS, but they can reduce visual quality, so balance is key

**VRAM - Virtual RAM**
That’s your GPU’s memory, and it’s shown in the bottom-right corner of the settings menu. Just keep in mind—it’s an estimate and may not always be exact.

To track FPS and VRAM in real time, press `Windows + G` to open the built-in Windows Game Bar. Or use third-party tools like MSI Afterburner for more detail.

### Settings

**Window Mode** – *Fullscreen, Windowed, or Windowed Fullscreen*.
Fullscreen usually gives better quality while Windowed Fullscreen may give better performance.
***Impact:*** *Average*

**Resolution** – Lowering it boosts FPS, but things may look less crisp.
***Impact: High***

**VSync** – Prevents screen tearing, but can increase input lag. Turning it off can help FPS.
***Impact:*** *Average*

**FPS Limit** – Capping your frames can help with heat and power draw, especially on laptops.
***Impact:*** *Minimum*

**Hardware Lumen** – Disabling this can dramatically improve performance.
***Impact:*** *High*

**Presets** – Choose Low, Medium, High, or Epic. A quick way to adjust everything at once.
***Impact:*** *Varies*

**Resolution Scale** – A major performance saver. Lowering this reduces internal rendering resolution.
***Impact:*** *High*

**Anti-Aliasing** – Smooths out jagged edges. Turning it down can give a decent FPS bump.
***Impact:*** *Average*

**Post Processing** – Things like bloom and blur. Can be turned down for more frames.
***Impact:*** *Average*

**Shadow Quality** – Very demanding. Lowering it can give you a solid performance boost.
***Impact:*** *High*

**Global Illumination** – Controls how light bounces. Great visuals, but expensive to render.
***Impact:*** *High*

**Reflections** – Lower this for a noticeable FPS gain.
***Impact:*** *High*

**Texture Quality** – Tied to your GPU’s VRAM. Lower if you notice stuttering.
***Impact:*** *Average*

**Visual Effects** – Reduce for smoother busy scenes.
***Impact:*** *Average*

**Foliage** – Controls grass and trees. Lower can help in outdoor areas.
***Impact:*** *Average*

**Shading** – Impacts lighting realism. Lowering helps performance.
***Impact:*** *High*
