Armor
=====

Armor is a form of apparel a player can equip to their body. It is the most effective way of defending against in-coming physical damage in Cornucopias. Different armor sets will have varying levels of protection based upon the armor quality. Armor quality depends upon the [resources](/gameplay/gameplay-pc/resources) used and skill of the Crafter.

Armor is spelt in American English for the COPIWiki, it can also be spelt Armour - [see Language, syntax and spellings.](/the-company/compliance/copiwiki-language-and-dictionaries)

### Obtaining

Armor can be obtained by:

* Purchase.
* Looted from themed zones.
* Rewards for quests.
* Crafted by players.

Unique armor sets can be purchased as NFTs.
