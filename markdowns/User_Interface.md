User Interface
==============

The players command center is in the form of a holographic menu display watch, which has a list of menu options the player can call up during the game.

Players will be able to purchase unique [NFTs](/blockchain/player-owned-assets) for their menu displays in the form of hologram glasses, smartphones and magic orbs.
