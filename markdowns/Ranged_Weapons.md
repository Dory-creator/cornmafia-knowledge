Ranged Weapons
==============

Ranged weapons are a form of equipment and are the most effective way of dealing damage at a distance to creatures and [livestock](/gameplay/gameplay-pc/resources/livestock) in Cornucopias. They come in the form of bows and arrows. Players can craft them by using resources gathered from themed zones.

Unique weapons can be purchased as NFTs.

Bows
----

Bows are ranged weapons used during ranged combat.
