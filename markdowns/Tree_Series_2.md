Tree Series 2
=============

​The Tree Series 2, created in partnership with Veritree, is a unique and environmentally conscious way to bridge the gap between the metaverse and real-life.

#### Utility​

​The Tree Series 2 has the following utility within Cornucopias

* One or more **real-life trees** will be planted around the world when the Asset is purchased and for ongoing second market sales.​
* Additional benefits to Tree Series 2 holders will be announced in the future.​
* As an [Asset](/blockchain/player-owned-assets), it will form part of your Cornucopias **Asset collection****.​**
* **You own the Asset and will be able to sell or trade it.​**
