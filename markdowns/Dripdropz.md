DripDropz
=========

Cardano Service Provider

![](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4046923609-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPQmCVki2WHg9QcW9pdrX%252Fuploads%252FfvbIyzf8PVHK5CMpxK9Y%252Fdripdropz.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Df25db4b9-632d-4267-b1e1-41d1685f9a97&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=eccbe488&sv=2)

On 20th August 2022 we launched our [NFT Staking program](/gameplay/about-the-game/stake-and-earn/staking) with the help of DripDropz.

Cornucopias generously offered 2 Million COPI tokens blockchain wide, via DripDropz. To claim your share, anyone could just head over to DripDropz and the tokens were yours to claim!

This is just the first project that both of our teams are worked on together, so keep an eye out for even more great opportunities to come!​
