Calido City
===========

Calido City can be found in the Calido Valley Mega Dome on the "Outer Rim" of Cornucopias.

This is the area in front of the racing tracks where players can meet, go shopping, have a look around the various attractions and when ready enter the [Calido Valley Raceway](/gameplay/mega-dome-calido-valley/calido-valley-resort/calido-valley-raceway) and race around the tracks in the invigorating and fast paced Cornucopias Racing League.

![](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4046923609-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPQmCVki2WHg9QcW9pdrX%252Fuploads%252FM4nxiAawOY0dIM1AoUWy%252FCalidoValley_4K.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D22b3d827-1166-4d52-aacc-e0ae44dddee1&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=117cb7f0&sv=2)

Calido City doubled the size of the explorable map of the Calido Valley Megadome

**Calido City** was introduced to [Calido Valley](/gameplay/mega-dome-calido-valley/calido-valley-resort) in the "City Slickers" release in December 2024. Found within Calido City is the first Aero Trails course - a purpose built face paced times mini-game where players have to fly through rings found within the City. Various fastest times competitions have been held where the fastest times are rewarded with $COPI.

The Nexus Express allows players to trade in their In Game Credits for various In Game items such as clothes and food.

FEATURES:

* **Calido City:** See the other side of Calido for the first time. Still highly a work-in-progress and will be updated over time to support many game features.
* **Aero Trails Game Mechanic:** A jetpack flying course through rings in Calido City that starts near the airport entrance and takes you to heights in Calido City. Compete with others for the best time. Jetpack required.
* **Early Crowd and NPC System:** bring Calido City to life.
* **Calido International Airport:** can be partially visited and still WIP
* **Early Banking functionality:** collect credit chips and deposit them into your account! Various ATM machines are available around Calido City.
* **Nexus Express:** is available in Calido City.
