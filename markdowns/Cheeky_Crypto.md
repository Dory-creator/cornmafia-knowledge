Cheeky Crypto
=============

YouTube Influencers

Cheeky Crypto is a successful podcast on YouTube and Twitter that provide information on cryptocurrency Investor's & Traders.

![](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4046923609-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPQmCVki2WHg9QcW9pdrX%252Fuploads%252FljCMuIeOcehG09wWCh4L%252Fimage.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D4f57f0a4-a9a1-4765-a504-92ac4fc9dcb5&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=53ac2347&sv=2)

From Twitter Bio: "Successful Crypto Investor's & Traders. Focused On Finding Undervalued Altcoins That Build Generational Level Wealth - Always Take Profits".

The Cheeky Crypto partnership was the first NFT collaboration with an external company and Cornucopias.

The collaboration Racing Suit NFT sale was completed in December 2022 and was talked about on [COPICafe](/gameplay/community/copicafe-archive-videos) Episode 34 with the two Cheeky Crypto hosts Chris and Nick.

The racing suit will be able to be worn whilst racing in the Cornucopias Racing League.
