RareBloom 2022
==============

![](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4046923609-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPQmCVki2WHg9QcW9pdrX%252Fuploads%252F4UJZaF7rnVfDKq9HGya4%252Fimage.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D9ff6ffa5-8c73-4d15-98a4-41d29bc683d5&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=b7f2754&sv=2)

In October 2022 the 4x Founders of Cornucopias attended the new annual [RareBloom](https://www.rarebloom.io/) event in Denver, Colorado - held at the Gaylord Rockies Hotel.

This was a smaller event than the [cNFTCon](/gameplay/community/status-updates-archive/status-updates-2022/october-2022/cnftcon-2022) event earlier in the week and Cornucopias did not have a [booth](https://twitter.com/RareBloomEvent).

Instead this was more of a networking event with panels ongoing for the 2x day event.

Some of the highlights are as follows:

* The 4x Founders visited every booth (approx. 50) and spoke to everyone.
* Charles Hoskinson CEO of IOG attended the event along with other IOG and Cardano Foundation members
* Josh and Rob recorded a Special Edition of COPICafe on a quiet floor above the conference. The episode was hosted by [Farid of dApp Central](https://www.youtube.com/c/DappCentral) and [Pritesh of Kaizen Crypto](https://www.youtube.com/c/KaizenCrypto).

![](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4046923609-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPQmCVki2WHg9QcW9pdrX%252Fuploads%252FOOb11C6kOspcrgGlKpAo%252Fimage.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D0107c36b-cdc8-48c5-aaa8-85f98bcaaa60&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=3f1a955&sv=2)

Rob and Josh with Farid (left) and Pritesh (right)

It was a great event to chat to other project CEOs and a Music Partnership with [NEWM](https://newm.io/) was announced. Watch Episode 27 of [COPICafe](/gameplay/community/copicafe-archive-videos) to see the announcement and to find out more.
