Cross Chain Technology
======================

Cross chain technology allows transmissions of value and information between different block-chain networks which ensures interoperability.

Infinity Rising will be able to read blockchain wallets and view Player Owned Assets on the following chains:

* [BASE Chain](/the-company/technology/cross-chain-technology/base-chain)
* [Cardano](/the-company/technology/cross-chain-technology/cardano-blockchain) Blockchain
