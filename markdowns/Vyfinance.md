VyFinance
=========

You will need a Cardano wallet such as Nami or Eternl to use VyFinance

You can farm with the Cardano version of the [$COPI](/blockchain/usdcopi-token) token on VyFinance.

The first staking program on VyFinance was launched on 3rd March 2023 and was a huge success with participation leaping all other staking programs (including VyFinances own).
