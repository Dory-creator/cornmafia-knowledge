Tree Series 1
=============

The Tree Series 1, created in partnership with [Veritree](/the-company/partners/partners-metaverse/veritree), is a unique and environmentally conscious way to bridge the gap between the metaverse and real-life.

#### **Colors and Rarities**

The table below shares the rarity and distribution method

**Good to know:** The current Asset design has a Rarity Status, this is what is referred to in the chart below.​

1. **Common** - Accounts for **50%** of all Asset batches
2. **Uncommon** - Accounts for **25%** of all Asset batches
3. **Rare** -Accounts for **15%** of all Asset batches
4. **Legendary** - Accounts for **7.5%** of all Asset batches
5. **Mythic** - Accounts for **2.5%** of all Asset batches

#### Utility

The Tree Series 1 has the following utility within Cornucopias:

* One or more **real-life trees** will be planted around the world when the Asset is purchased and for ongoing second market sales.
* Additional benefits to Tree Series 1 holders will be announced in the future.
* As an [Asset](/blockchain/player-owned-assets), it will form part of your Cornucopias **Asset collection**.
* **You own the Asset and will be able to sell or trade it****.**
