Burger Barn Deluxe #1 Hat
=========================

The Burger Barn Deluxe #1 Hat launched on 1st November 2024

Are you ready for a flippin’ good time? Burger Barn Deluxe Hat No. 1 – a playful, eye-catching hat that transforms a burger, fries, and drink into wearable fun.

Perfect for parties, promotions, or just showing off your love for Burger Barn, this hat is sure to spark smiles and turn heads wherever you go!

A snapshot $0.354 @ 1200UTC on 1st November 2024 was used in calculating the price of the Assets in ADA (₳).

**Minted:** 1st November 2024

**Delivery Method:** via Cornucopias internally built marketplace

**Maximum Total Supply (multi-chain):** 7,600

#### Rarities

* **Fixed Rarity:** Rare

#### Utility

The Burger Barn Deluxe #1 Hat has the following utility within Cornucopias

* It is a Helmet you can wear on your Avatars head
* It has to be equipped to your Avatar to enable its use in-game
* As an [Asset,](/blockchain/player-owned-assets) it will form part of your Cornucopias Asset **collection.**
* **You own the** Asset **asset and will be able to sell or trade it.**
