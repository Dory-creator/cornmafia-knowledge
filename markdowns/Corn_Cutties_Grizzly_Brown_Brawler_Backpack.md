Corn Cutties - Grizzly Brown Brawler Backpack
=============================================

The Corn Cutties - Grizzly Brown Brawler Backpack launched on 1st November 2024

Introducing the Grizzly Brown Brawler Backpack by Corn Cuties—a perfect mix of cuddly charm with a rugged edge!

This lovable pack may look soft, but it’s built to handle your adventures. With moderate storage capacity, it's ideal for carrying your daily essentials while adding a playful twist to your style. Featuring the signature Corn Cuties design with paw accents, the Brown Brawler is the perfect companion for those who like their adventures cute, but tough.

A snapshot $0.0278 @ 1200UTC on 1st November 2024 was used in calculating the price of the Assets in $COPI.

**Minted:** 1st November 2024

**Delivery Method:** via Cornucopias internally built marketplace

**Maximum Total Supply (multi-chain):** 3,000

#### Rarities

* **Fixed Rarity:** Rare

#### Utility

The Grizzly Brown Brawler has the following utility within Cornucopias

* It is a Backpack and is used to extend the amount of inventory items you can carry around Cornucopias
* It has to be equipped to your Avatar to enable its use in-game
* As an [Asset](/blockchain/player-owned-assets), it will form part of your Cornucopias **Asset collection.**
* **You own the Asset and will be able to sell or trade it.**
