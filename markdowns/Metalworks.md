Metalworks
==========

The workshop necessary for processing ore into metal concentrate.

Metalworks can be found in every [district](/gameplay/themed-zones/sectors/land-sector-districts) and include a grinder, shaking table, and smelting furnace.

Grinding
--------

The workstation used to remove waste rock from ore and separate valuable mineral.

Smelting Furnace
----------------

An industrial oven used to heat [ore](/gameplay/gameplay-pc/resources/metal) in order to convert it into metal concentrate.
