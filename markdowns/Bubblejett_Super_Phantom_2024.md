Bubblejett Super Phantom 2024
=============================

The Bubblejett Super Phantom launched on 9th November 2024 and is part of the Flying Vehicle Series

The Bubblejett Super Phantom is an elite S-class racing vehicle built for those who crave speed and precision. Powered by cutting-edge Gen-Core technology, this high-performance craft features twin jet engines that deliver unparalleled thrust, enabling rapid acceleration and tight manoeuvrability.

The Super Phantom’s sleek, aerodynamic design reduces drag and enhances stability, making it a top choice for competitive racers.

The Bubblejett Super Phantom is also a flying vehicle that is used to travel from the Sector Hub to the [Themed Zones](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/gameplay/themed-zones) or the [City](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/gameplay/city-life). It can also be used for racing and is a Racing Class S vehicle.

#### Utility

The Bubblejett Super Phantom 2024 has the following utility within Cornucopias:

* It is a flying vehicle and is used to **travel** from the Sector Hub to the [Themed Zones](/gameplay/themed-zones) or the [City](/gameplay/city-life).
* It can be used for[**racing**](/gameplay/gameplay-pc/custom-domes/custom-dome-game-templates-pvp/racing-bubblejett-and-javelin)in a [Mega Dome](/gameplay/gameplay-pc/custom-domes/mega-domes) such as [Calido Valley](/gameplay/mega-dome-calido-valley).
* You will be able to [**rent**](/gameplay/themed-zones/transport/flying-vehicles/flying-vehicle-renting) your Asset to other players to play or race with.
* You will be able to **stake** your Asset.
* It **looks** great in your garage and you will be able to show it off to your friends when they visit your Sector Hub.
* As an [Asset](/blockchain/player-owned-assets), it will form part of your Cornucopias **Asset collection.**
* **You own the Asset and will be able to sell or trade it.**
