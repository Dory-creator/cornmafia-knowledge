Kargo Outfitters - Titan XL Backpack
====================================

The Kargo Outfitters - Titan XL Backpack launched on 1st November 2024

Introducing the Titan XL Cargo Backpack by Kargo Outfitters – the ultimate solution for heavy-duty transport. Built for adventurers, explorers, and traders, the Titan X is designed to carry more with maximum durability and efficiency.

Its rugged construction features reinforced frames and premium materials to withstand the toughest conditions. With multiple compartments and easy-access pockets, organizing your gear has never been easier. Whether you're navigating challenging terrain or embarking on long journeys, the Titan X provides unmatched storage capacity and reliable comfort, making it the perfect companion for all your cargo needs.

A snapshot $0.0278 @ 1200UTC on 1st November 2024 was used in calculating the price of the Assets in $COPI.

#### Utility

The Titan XL has the following utility within Cornucopias

* It is a Backpack and is used to extend the amount of inventory items you can carry around Cornucopias
* It has to be equipped to your Avatar to enable its use in-game
* As an [Asset](/blockchain/player-owned-assets), it will form part of your Cornucopias **Asset collection.**
* **You own the Asset and will be able to sell or trade it.**
