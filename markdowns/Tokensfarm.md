TokensFarm
==========

You can farm with the Binance BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) version of the [$COPI](/blockchain/usdcopi-token) token on TokensFarm.

![](https://copiwiki.cornucopias.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4046923609-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPQmCVki2WHg9QcW9pdrX%252Fuploads%252FHaEszzKC41DaameSu8Wa%252Ftokensfarmlogojpg.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Da7c6f4f7-37f4-422b-93a1-7b2d7877aef0&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=e0180017&sv=2)

The first staking program on TokensFarm was launched on 23rd December 2021 and was a huge success.

The second staking program was launched on 10th February 2022.

A third staking program was launched on 14th May 2022.

A fourth staking program was launched on 15th September 2022.

A fifth staking program was launched on 22nd February 2023.
