Calido Valley Pavilion
======================

The Calido Valley Pavilion can be found in the Calido Valley [Mega Dome](/gameplay/gameplay-pc/custom-domes/mega-domes).

This is an area you get to via the Calido Valley Resort lifts near the Grand Valley Hotel. It is a shopping mall that has a number of attractions including the mini game Rumble Ball. It is also the place you go if you want to race in the fast paced Cornucopias Racing League.
